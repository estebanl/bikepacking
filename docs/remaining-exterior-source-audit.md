## Work still needed

1. Implement the two connector families with explicit host-generation, fixed/removable, and bare-arch/rack conflicts. Confirm whether the current January 2026 CargoPack Top Bag is the Gen 2 host named on v2 connector pages before enabling that attachment.
2. Build `tailfin-894177` only through an explicit bare Carbon/Alloy Arch configuration and the required arch dependencies. The catalog currently has complete rear rack systems and arch spare records; it does not provide a supported bare-arch conversion assembly. Do not convert a complete rack by silently removing its top stay or reusing its system mass.
3. Add bar-system replacement parts and mount options with source-supported host/socket relationships; retain unknown package counts and dimensions.
4. Resolve the selectable T-Hook and buckle kits to exact variants before modeling their geometry or enabling them on a host.
5. Add supported pannier replacements and spacers with replacement conflicts. Represent the 22 L SL/UD historical pannier generation before enabling its lower-spare set.
6. Defer Journey Fit Link placement and Carbon Arch Mini Bumper placement/count until the official source gives a usable mapped location and package scope.
7. Treat unknown or omitted metrics as null. Do not infer mass by subtraction from a host bag/rack; removed replacement-part mass is not published.
